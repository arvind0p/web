Success Story

Case Study

Improving Lead Follow-up Workflow
Project: Q1 2026 Duration: 2 Months Impact: High

The Problem

Leads were slipping through the cracks due to inconsistent follow-up processes. The team lacked a standardized system for tracking lead status, resulting in delayed responses and missed conversion opportunities. Communication gaps between sales team members led to duplicate efforts and client confusion.

The Solution

I implemented a structured tracking system using CRM tools and Google Sheets, establishing clear accountability for each lead. Created a standardized follow-up schedule with automated reminders, ensuring no lead went unattended. Introduced daily team syncs to review pipeline status and coordinate efforts, eliminating duplication and improving team communication.

The Results

Significantly improved response handling and follow-up consistency. Enhanced conversion flow through better lead nurturing and timely communication. Increased team accountability and collaboration, reducing duplicate efforts by over 60%. The new system became the standard workflow across the sales team, contributing to overall business growth and client satisfaction.
